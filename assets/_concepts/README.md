# How the gannet logo was chosen

A record, not live material. These are the raster drafts the mark and the
wordmark were chosen from on 21 Oct 2026. Nothing here ships: every shipped
asset comes from `../_gen/logo.py`. GPT Image billed US$0.29 for all of them.

## The mark

`01` to `08` - eight directions for the mark, from one batch (`runs.jsonl`):
head in profile, dive into a circle, origami, a `g` monogram, negative space,
the bird from below in flight, a splash badge and an eye drawn as a lens.
**`05-negative-space` was chosen, on navy rather than ink** (Dan: "05 on navy
blue"). It was the most recognisably a gannet and the strongest at 32 px.
`sheet-r1-with-small.webp` shows all eight with a 32 px row.

## The wordmark

A monospace wordmark (JetBrains Mono, as terraken's) and ten typefaces were
turned down first ("dont like the font"). Then two rounds of drafted lettering:

- `script/s01` to `s08` - italic connected scripts, after the Coca-Cola
  reference Dan gave. Turned down: "dont want italic".
- `script/g01` to `g08` - upright and geometric, following `s04`.
  **`g07-gold-eye` was chosen, without its gold dot** (Dan: "7 without the
  yellow dot"). `g04` was out because its joined `nn` reads as `gamet`.

`script/lockups.webp` and `script/lockups-geo.webp` show each round beside the
mark. The generator rebuilds `g07` from its measured proportions rather than
tracing it, so every bowl is a true circle and every stem is straight.
